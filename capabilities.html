Engineering Capabilities & Portfolio

TURNKEY EPC SOLUTIONS FROM 11KV TO 400KV

GK Power Transmission Company Pvt. Ltd provides full-scope turnkey solutions for EHV transmission lines, switchyards, transformer fire prevention, and electrical maintenance across India.

01 / Turnkey Switchyards

TURNKEY CONTRACTS FOR 132KV, 220KV & 400KV SWITCHYARDS

We deliver complete turnkey contracts for EHV switchyards and associated equipment with State Electricity Boards (SEBs), Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL), and defense utilities.

•

Design, Erection & Structure Mounting

Heavy civil foundations, tower gantry erection, busbar routing, earth mat layout calculations, and lightning arresters.

•

Equipment Supply & Interfacing

Supply and integration of high voltage disconnectors, current transformers, potential transformers, isolators, and auxiliary equipment.

•

Testing, Pre-Commissioning & Handover

Full pre-commissioning testing, primary & secondary injection tests, contact resistance measurement, and grid synchronization.

02 / Transformer Explosion Solutions

TRANSFORMER EXPLOSION & FIRE PREVENTION SOLUTIONS (NIFPS)

Power transformer fires can cause catastrophic damage, environmental devastation, and extensive blackouts. GK Power specializes in state-of-the-art Nitrogen Injection Fire Prevention Systems (NIFPS) adhering to CEA norms.

•

Fast Depressurization & Oil Drain Valve

Instantaneous activation upon arc detection, draining oil into containment pits before tank rupture occurs.

•

Nitrogen Purging & Stirring Action

High pressure nitrogen is injected into the bottom of the transformer, churning oil, displacing oxygen, and suppressing combustion within seconds.

03 / Transformers & Products

POWER TRANSFORMERS & ELECTRIC DISTRIBUTION TRANSFORMERS

GK Power Transmission Company manufactures, supplies, and overhauls high-grade Power Transformers and Electric Transformers engineered for rugged operational duty across Indian grids.

•

Power & Generating Transformers

Erection, oil filtration under high vacuum, degassing, dielectric breakdown voltage testing (BDV), and Tan Delta dissipation factor analysis.

•

Electric Distribution Transformers

Custom-built outdoor step-down transformers, OLTC/OCTC integration, conservator breather systems, and temperature indicator gauges.

04 / Transmission Corridor Works

ERECTION OF EHV TRANSMISSION LINES (11KV TO 400KV)

Execution of overhead extra-high-voltage transmission lines across difficult terrain, rivers, hills, and urban corridors with utmost safety standards.

•

Tower Foundation & Structural Assembly

Civil footing, stub setting, chimney casting, lattice tower member erection, and anti-climbing device installation.

•

Conductor Stringing & Sag-Tensioning

Tensioner-puller machinery stringing of ACSR/HTLS conductors, optical ground wire (OPGW), vibration dampers, and spacer placement.

05 / Overhauling & Repairs

REPAIRS, OVERHAULING OF CIRCUIT BREAKERS & SUB-STATION EQUIPMENTS

Our competent group of high-voltage certified engineers and technicians handle overhauling and rehabilitation of circuit breakers, power transformers, and switchyard equipment up to 400 KV.

•

SF6 & Vacuum Circuit Breakers

Contact timing tests, travel speed curves, dynamic resistance measurement (DRM), SF6 gas reclamation, and mechanical spring/pneumatic drive overhauls.

•

Sub-Station Equipments Reliability

Preventive maintenance, hot-line insulator washing, thermo-vision scanning, and life-extension refurbishment for aging grid bays.

06 / Protection & SCADA

SUB-STATION AUTOMATION, RELAY PANELS & SCADA (IEC 61850)

Providing comprehensive Protection Relay Panels, Control & Relay Panels (CRP), and Sub-Station Automation Systems to ensure fail-safe grid security and remote telemetry.

•

Numeric Protection & Relay Panels

Differential, distance protection, overcurrent, earth fault, breaker failure, and busbar protection schemes integrated into customized panels.

•

Sub-Station Automation & SCADA

Implementation of modern IEC 61850 protocols, fiber optic communication links, sequence-of-events recording, and remote bay control.
