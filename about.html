Company Heritage & Profile

OVER 16+ YEARS OF TRANSMISSION EXCELLENCE

We, G. K. Power Transmission Co. Pvt. Ltd. are one of the leading turnkey contractors & suppliers for EHV Lines and switchyard equipments from 11KV to 400KV and for various protection products with SEBs, MES, PGCIL, and central/state government organizations.

Who We Are

LEADING TURNKEY CONTRACTOR FOR EHV LINES & SWITCHYARDS

Incorporated under the Indian Companies Act in 2006, GK Power Transmission Company Private Limited has established itself as an indispensable engineering partner to Maharashtra State Electricity Boards (MSETCL, MSPGCL), Military Engineer Services (MES), and Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL).

We have a very good and competent group of engineers and technicians to handle any type of Erection, repairs, and overhauling of Circuit Breakers, Power Transformers, EHV Lines, and other equipment in the range of utilities up to 400 KV. We have also executed turnkey contracts for 132KV, 220KV & 400KV switchyards and associated equipment.
